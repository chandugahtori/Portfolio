Turning Curiosity Into Infrastructure

I'm Chandra Shekhar, a Computer Science graduate with a strong interest in DevOps, cloud computing, and software automation. I started this journey out of curiosity about how real-world applications are deployed and scaled—and that curiosity continues to drive me every day.

I enjoy bridging the gap between development and operations. Whether it's containerizing applications with Docker, building and deploying the Navix Grocery platform using React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Nginx, and Docker, or automating infrastructure with AWS and CI/CD pipelines, I enjoy building complete, production-ready solutions from development to deployment.

When I'm not writing YAML or Bash scripts, you'll find me on the sports field — I'm a national-level Kho-Kho player and former college team captain, which taught me leadership, discipline, and performing under pressure.
